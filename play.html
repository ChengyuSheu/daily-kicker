How to ride

All rotation is created at the moment you leave the snow. In the air you cannot start a spin — there is nothing to push against. You wind it up first, then pop.

1 · Wind up on the ground

A / Dcarve left or right — also coils a spin
↑ / ↓load a flip (front or back)
Q / Eload a cork (off-axis roll)

The coil readout shows all three. They fade slowly if you let go, so a flip you loaded a second ago is still there when the lip arrives.

2 · Leave the ground

SpaceOllie — pops, and spends every coil into rotation
—a kicker launches you the same way, using whatever you have loaded

3 · In the air: change your shape, not your momentum

ShiftGrab — tucks you up. Flips and corks go ~2.5× faster
SSprawl — opens you out and slows a spin to check it

A grab does not speed up a flat spin: folding over moves mass away from the vertical axis, so yaw actually slows a little. Standing tall is the fastest spin there is. That is real, and it is why riders tuck for corked rotations and open up to bring one back down.
