CARVE & COIL

How to ride

All rotation is created at the moment you leave the snow. In the air you cannot start a spin — there is nothing to push against. You wind it up first, then pop.

1 · Wind up on the ground

Carve padslide your left thumb — carving also coils a spin. The bar behind the thumb fills as it winds
⟳ Fliphold to load a flip

The coils fade slowly when you let go, so a flip you loaded a second ago is still there when the lip arrives.

2 · Leave the ground

OLLIEpops, and spends every coil into rotation
Tap the snowsame as Ollie — quicker when your thumb is already there
A kickerlaunches you the same way, using whatever you have loaded

3 · In the air: change your shape

✊ Grabhold — tucks you up. Flips and corks go ~2.5× faster
Releaseopens you out and slows the rotation to check it

A grab does not speed up a flat spin: folding over moves mass away from the vertical axis, so yaw actually slows a little. Standing tall is the fastest spin there is. That is real, and it is why riders tuck for corked rotations and open up to bring one back down.

Land square to stomp it. The steeper the pitch the faster you go — the HUD shows the grade, blue through black.
